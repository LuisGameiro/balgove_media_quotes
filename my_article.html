European partners accelerate air defence deliveries ahead of winter

KYIV — Ukraine and its European partners have finalized an expedited schedule for delivering interceptor missiles and mobile radar units before temperatures drop below freezing across the country's eastern and southern regions, officials confirmed on Thursday.

Speaking after a joint defense ministry briefing in Kyiv, Defense Minister Rustem Umerov said “Every additional air defence battery deployed this month directly protects critical energy infrastructure and civilian heating networks across our cities.”

European logistics hubs have already processed the first shipment of interceptors under the new bilateral framework agreed earlier in November, according to defense officials familiar with the transfer schedule.

«Ми також збільшуємо власне виробництво боєприпасів та дронів-перехоплювачів на двадцять відсотків цього кварталу,» заявив Денис Шмигаль під час засідання уряду.

“Our joint production lines are operating around the clock to ensure uninterrupted supplies of artillery ammunition and spare parts throughout the winter campaign,” added Prime Minister Denys Shmyhal.
